## Documentation Approach

Each writeup aims to provide:

- **Reconnaissance & Enumeration** — Identifying exposed services, attack surfaces, and potential entry points.
- **Technical Analysis** — Understanding discovered weaknesses and their underlying causes.
- **Exploitation & Validation** — Documenting practical techniques and their observed results.
- **Key Takeaways** — Capturing technical insights and lessons learned.

The documentation structure may vary depending on the nature and complexity of each challenge.

## Disclaimer

All writeups are based on authorized TryHackMe lab environments and are intended strictly for educational purposes, security research, and knowledge sharing.
